# Art prompts

Almost every image on the site is now a real HBO still, a frame from HBO's *Histories & Lore* animations, or official licensed book art (credits are in each `public/*/got/CREDITS.md`). The prompts below cover the few images where none of those exist.

Generate an image in Grok, save it at the target path, then ask Claude to wire it in (or update the path in the named data file).

A small Thenn settlement of low stone-and-sod houses with smoke rising, crude bronze-working hearths glowing, a few fur-clad, shaven-headed men and women carrying bronze-headed spears and axes. ### Ice-river clans
Target: public/north/got/ice-river.jpg  (1248x832, 3:2; then point north.ts `ice-river` at it)
Photoreal cinematic wide shot, 3:2, no text. A poor, hungry Free Folk clan camped on a frozen river in the far north-west beyond the Wall at dusk: patched hide tents and bone-framed shelters on the ice, a small driftwood fire, gaunt men and women in ragged, layered furs and sealskin, fishing through holes cut in the ice with bone hooks and spears. Sledges and a few thin dogs. The river runs between low, snow-buried hills and dark leafless trees; the sky is iron grey. Wary and half-starved, with a hint of menace (a few gnawed bones by the fire, nothing gory). Game of Thrones HBO production design, desaturated cold palette, natural lighting, no text or watermark.


## Grok prompts: Rebellion tab

### Gulltown
Target: public/roberts/got/gulltown.jpg
Photoreal cinematic wide shot, 16:9, 1280x720. Gulltown, the walled port city of the Vale on the Bay of Crabs in Westeros, during Robert's Rebellion. It is grey morning. Pale stone walls and towers rise straight from the harbour. Grey slate roofs climb a hill toward a small keep flying a pale-blue banner with a silver falcon under a crescent moon (House Arryn). Fishing cogs and a few war galleys are tied up at stone quays. Gulls hang over the masts. Behind the town, the snow-capped Mountains of the Moon fade into mist. Smoke rises from one breached gate, and a small band of armoured men in Baratheon gold with black stags climbs the wall on ladders. The palette is muted blue-grey and gold, with the natural light of a period drama and the texture of 35mm film. No text, no logos, no modern objects.

### Sisterton (Three Sisters)
Target: public/roberts/got/sisterton.jpg
Photoreal cinematic wide shot, 16:9, 1280x720. Sisterton on Sweetsister, one of the Three Sisters islands in the Bite of Westeros. It is a poor, grim smugglers' port at dusk under a storm sky. A squat grey stone keep, the Sealord's Tower of House Borrell, stands on a rocky headland above a cramped harbour of tarred wooden houses on stilts. Small fishing boats are drawn up on shingle. Cold waves break white against black rocks. A single small open fishing boat with a patched sail comes in through rough water, carrying a cloaked young northern lord. The mood is bleak, wet and windswept, with the colour grading of a period drama. No text, no logos, no modern objects.


## Optional upgrades (keeps2)

These two cards now show official FFG card art, but only small card scans exist (about 250 px wide). Use these prompts if you want a sharper image. Replace the file in place.

### The Arbor
Target: public/keeps/got/the-arbor.jpg
Photoreal cinematic establishing shot, 3:2 landscape (1248x832), no text. A golden island off the southwestern tip of Westeros in late-afternoon summer light. Terraced hills of grapevines roll down to a busy harbor on the Redwyne Straits, full of tall-masted war galleys and merchant cogs with burgundy sails. On a rise above the harbor stands the Redwyne castle: pale honey-colored stone, square towers and battlements, and burgundy banners with a blue bunch of grapes. The town below has warm stone houses with red-tiled roofs. Turquoise water, a light haze on the horizon toward Oldtown, and a Mediterranean wine-country feel. The castle sits in the upper-middle third of the frame.

### Torrhen's Square
Target: public/keeps/got/torrhens-square.jpg
Photoreal cinematic establishing shot, 3:2 landscape (1248x832), no text. A stern grey stone castle in the wooded North of Westeros, on the northern shore of a large still lake. Thirty-foot curtain walls have square towers at each corner and a heavy square keep. Pine and oak woods, the wolfswood, rise behind it, and a river runs south out of the lake. The light is overcast and cool, with low mist on the water. Banners show three green sentinel trees on white (House Tallhart). The castle sits centered in the middle third of the frame, mirrored in the lake.
