# Heroes images: sources

Hero cards are portrait images, 1050×1400 or close to it, matching the other files in this folder. The tab banner `hero.jpg` is 1280×720.

This table covers the five files added in the September 2026 art sweep. The other portraits in this folder are HBO stills of the actors from *Game of Thrones*. They were checked by eye, and each one shows the right character.

Rights: HBO owns the stills and the *Histories & Lore* frames. Fantasy Flight Games and the credited artist own the card art. All images are used here with credit in a non-commercial fan project, under the same fair-use basis as the fan wikis they were found on.

## Other options found

- Aemon the Dragonknight also appears in the *Histories & Lore* short "The Kingsguard" (File:Aemon_the_Dragonknight.png, 768×633, a sepia sketch with title text over it). The Aemon art on A Wiki of Ice and Fire is fan art (Chillyraven, novembermorgon, Jota Saraiva) or has an unclear source, so none of it was used.
- Garlan Tyrell also has a second FFG card by Aaron Miller, showing him on horseback holding a rose: https://awoiaf.westeros.org/images/0/01/Garlan_Tyrell.jpg
- Daemon Blackfyre also appears in "The Death of Kings" (File:Daemon Blackfyre.png, a close-up sketch) and in "The Blackfyres" (File:Knighting of Daemon Blackfyre.jpg).
