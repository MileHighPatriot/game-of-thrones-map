# Atlas seat and city images: sources

These replace the AI art in `public/seats/`. Wherever possible they are establishing shots from HBO's *Game of Thrones*. Lannisport, White Harbor and Sunspear are never shown in live action, so they use HBO's animated *Histories & Lore* shorts. Each image was cropped to 3:2 and resized to 1248×832 JPEG. Tarth uses `keeps/got/tarth.jpg`, and the other castles on the atlas use their `keeps/got/` stills (see `public/keeps/got/CREDITS.md`).
