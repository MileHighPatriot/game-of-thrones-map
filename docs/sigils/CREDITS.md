# House sigils

Westerosi coats of arms for Stark, Lannister, Baratheon, Greyjoy, Tyrell, Martell, Arryn, Tully, Bolton, Frey, Baelish, and Targaryen are by [FDRMRZUSA](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:FDRMRZUSA) on Wikimedia Commons, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Blazons follow [A Wiki of Ice and Fire](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/) and the Citadel heraldry lists. Factions with no Westerosi arms use the emblems those cultures actually fly or carve.
