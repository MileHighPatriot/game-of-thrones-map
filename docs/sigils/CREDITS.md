# House sigils

House Stark uses the HBO show arms: a snarling grey direwolf head on white over green, matching the banners in the series and the Wiki of Westeros.

Westerosi coats of arms for Lannister, Baratheon, Greyjoy, Tyrell, Martell, Arryn, Tully, Bolton, Frey, Baelish, and Targaryen are by [FDRMRZUSA](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:FDRMRZUSA) on Wikimedia Commons, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Other blazons follow [A Wiki of Ice and Fire](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/) and the Citadel heraldry lists. Factions with no Westerosi arms use the emblems those cultures actually fly or carve.
