# The Dragons — artwork

The register portraits and the page banner live in `art/` and were made for this site.
Landscape cards are 1248×832. The banner is 1280×720. Show stills that remain, used for
lairs and for how dragons die, are in `got/` and credited in `got/CREDITS.md`. A missing
file still falls back to `pending.svg`.
