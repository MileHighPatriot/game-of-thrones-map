# The Dragons — artwork

Show stills are in `got/` and credited in `got/CREDITS.md`. The files in `art/` are original
images made for this site, for dragons and relics the shows never filmed. Landscape cards are
1248×832. The hero and the "How they die" tiles are 1280×720. A missing file still falls back
to `pending.svg`.
