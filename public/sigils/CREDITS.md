# House sigils

The great and major houses (Stark, Lannister, Baratheon, Stannis Baratheon, Targaryen, Greyjoy, Tyrell, Martell, Arryn, Tully, Bolton, Frey, Baelish) and the Faith of the Seven use the HBO show heraldry in [`got/`](got/CREDITS.md). That file lists every image with its exact source URL and license (© HBO, via the Wiki of Westeros, fair dealing, not Creative Commons).

The older Wikimedia and hand-made versions of these house sigils have been removed; only the files named in `src/lib/banners.ts` remain.

The Night's Watch, free folk, White Walkers, Dothraki, Great Masters, Braavos, Pentos, Qarth, and the ruins of Valyria have no Westerosi arms in the show, so they use small hand-drawn emblems made for this atlas (`nightswatch.svg`, `free-folk.svg` and so on).
